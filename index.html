What this is

Every six hours Claude reads a set of news feeds, picks the new stories, writes a short summary in its own words and gives each one a goodness score from 0 to 10. The full story always stays with the original publisher: tap Read at source to open it.

Stories older than 10 days drop off. Saved stories and the ones you've read are remembered on this device only.

The goodness score

The score rates how positive the news is for people, animals or the planet. It weighs how many lives it touches, how lasting the change is, and whether it already happened or is only a promise.

Sources

Summaries are kept short so the publisher gets your visit.

On your phone

On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app (or ⋮ then Add to Home screen). It then opens like an app and keeps the last stories for offline reading.

Prefer a news reader? Subscribe to the RSS feed, which carries stories scoring 6 or more.
